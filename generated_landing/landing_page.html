FAQ (the stuff you’ll ask before trusting a playbook)

Is this just a list of prompts? ›

No. It’s a directory of workflows—versioning/testing/context patterns with inputs/outputs, adoption steps, expected outcomes, and rationale.

Will this work with local models? ›

Yes. Playbooks are designed to be model-agnostic where possible, and tagged by model type/toolchain so you can filter for local-first workflows.

How does this help with frozen or unfinished outputs? ›

Playbooks include operational guardrails—stop conditions, retry rules, and validation checks—so stalls are detected and handled predictably.

Can I keep everything in git? ›

That’s the point. Templates are repo-ready artifacts (Markdown/YAML/prompt files) meant for PR review, diffs, and rollback.

How do you prevent low-quality submissions? ›

Submissions follow a structured format and are improved through voting + guided feedback, rather than unmoderated prompt dumping.
