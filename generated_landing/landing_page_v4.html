Shipping day shouldn’t feel like roulette

If you’re packing samples at midnight, the real tax is uncertainty: labeling, documentation, and carrier rules that change by product type and destination. One “wrong” shipment can mean delays, returns, or a shop headache that makes sampling even less viable. This checklist is designed for small-batch reality: do the minimum that keeps you safe this week, then scale up when you’re ready.
